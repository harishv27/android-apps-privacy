Roomsum: deleting your data

Roomsum has no accounts. A room's data is stored with Google Firebase so that roommates' phones stay in sync. You can remove your own data from inside the app, or ask us to do it.

To delete your data

  • Room tab > Delete my data replaces your name and UPI ID in every room on your phone with "Former roommate", deletes your anonymous sign-in and clears the app on your phone. Leave room only removes the room from your phone. Expenses you took part in stay in the room, without your name, so your roommates' balances stay correct.
  • Or uninstall Roomsum: Android removes all of the app's data with it.
