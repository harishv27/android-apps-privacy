Roomsum: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Roomsum is a shared expense tracker for roommates and flatmates made by Harish V (contact: harishvelayutham.dev@gmail.com).

The short version

Roomsum keeps a room's expenses with Google Firebase so roommates' phones stay in sync. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, asks for no location, contacts or camera access, and never sells or shares your data.

What Roomsum stores

  • Your display name and the other roommates in a room (people without a phone are just a name).
  • Expenses, payments between roommates, shopping list items and recurring bills that you or your roommates add: amounts, notes, categories, dates, who paid and how it was split.
  • An optional UPI ID, only if you enter one, so roommates can pay you from the settle-up screen.
  • An anonymous sign-in ID created by Firebase Authentication. It is not linked to your name, phone number, email or Google account.
  • A copy of the room's data on your phone so the app works offline; the same data lives in the room's database on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which every phone in the room reads and writes.
  • The room's data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), sent over encrypted connections, and cached on your phone so the app works offline. Everyone in a room can see that room's data. Android app backup is turned off for Roomsum.

What Roomsum does not do

  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, microphone, phone number or email address, and it has no account or password. Scanning a room's QR code uses Google's code scanner, which does not give Roomsum access to your camera or pictures.
  • It does not process payments. The UPI button only opens a UPI app you already have, with the amount filled in.
  • It does not sell your data or share it with anyone other than Google Firebase, which stores it for us.
  • It does send a room's data to Google Firebase (and receives it back) to keep the roommates' phones in sync. Google acts as our service provider for this and the data is not used for advertising.
  • It does not use advertising, tracking, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Permissions

  • Internet: to sync a room with Google Firebase.
  • Network state: to show the Offline banner when there is no connection.
  • Read Google services configuration: added by Google Play services (Firebase) so the SDK can read its configuration. It is not a runtime permission and shows nothing to the user.

Who can see your data

Everyone in a room can see that room's data. Only people who have the room code (or its QR code) can join. Someone who has the code can also see the list of people in the room (names, and UPI IDs if shared) so they can pick which one is them; they cannot see expenses until they join.

Third-party services

Roomsum uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) to store and sync a room. Google processes the data for us and its own privacy policy applies to that processing. Roomsum uses no other third-party service. Scanning a QR code uses Google's code scanner provided by Google Play services.

Payments

Roomsum does not process payments. The UPI buttons open a UPI app you already have (such as PhonePe, Google Pay or Paytm) with the amount filled in. Payments are made and handled entirely by that app.

Reports and exports

PDF reports and CSV files are created on your phone and shared by you through Android's share sheet. Roomsum does not send them anywhere.

Deleting your data

Room tab > Delete my data replaces your name and UPI ID in the room with "Former roommate", deletes your anonymous sign-in and clears the app on your phone. Leave room only removes the room from your phone. Expenses you took part in stay in the room, without your name, so your roommates' balances stay correct.

  • Uninstalling Roomsum removes the app and its copy of the data from your phone but not what is stored in the room's database, so use Delete my data first.
  • To ask for deletion without the app (for example after losing your phone), write to harishvelayutham.dev@gmail.com with the room name or code. We will remove your name and UPI ID from that room within 30 days.

Children

Roomsum is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
